1. Who we are

Littleverse is made by piney (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at piney91026@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Littleverse app for Android (from Google Play), the web version at csay1212.github.io/littleverse, and that web version when it’s added to an iPhone Home Screen. Where they differ, we say so.

2. What stays on your device

Everything you make in Littleverse is saved in the app’s own storage on your device (the browser storage known as IndexedDB and local storage). That includes:

  • Your universes: the names, dates, letters, notes and other words you write, and your choices of characters, colours, worlds and songs.
  • Photos and songs you add to a universe.
  • Gifts you receive: universes from gift files you open.
  • Settings: the name you enter in You, and whether sound effects and haptics are on.
  • Progress: passport stamps, stardust, stickers and other game progress.
  • In the Android app only: whether you own Remove ads, and your ad consent choice where that applies (saved on your device by Google’s consent form).

We don’t have a copy of any of this, and we can’t see it.

One thing outside our control: if Android’s own device backup is turned on, Android may include Littleverse’s data in the backup it keeps in your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is managed in your phone’s settings. We can’t access it.

3. Gift files

“Send as a gift” packs one universe, with its words, photos and songs, into a single .littleverse file and opens your device’s share sheet. You choose where it goes: a messaging app, email, a cloud drive or anything else. That service handles the file under its own privacy policy. Littleverse doesn’t upload the file anywhere, and we never receive a copy.

Anyone who has a gift file can open it and see everything inside, so only send it to people you’d trust with its contents. Once a file is sent, we can’t recall or delete it.

Universes are often about other people. Please only add photos and details that you’re comfortable sharing with the person you’re sending it to, and that the people in them would be happy for you to share.

When you open a gift file someone sent you, it’s copied into Littleverse on your device and stays there until you delete it.

4. Photos, songs, microphone, calendar

Photos and songs

When you add a photo or a song, your device’s own picker opens, and Littleverse can only see the files you choose. They’re copied into the app’s storage on your device and are only shared if you send a gift file that contains them.

Microphone (optional)

In the film’s cake scene you can tap the flames, or tap Blow into the mic and blow at your screen. Only then does Littleverse ask for the microphone, and your device asks for your permission first.

While the microphone is on, the app checks how loud the sound is, many times a second, to tell when you’re blowing. The sound is never recorded, saved, played back or sent anywhere. Listening stops when you tap Stop listening or leave the film. If you’d rather not, tap the flames instead. You can turn the permission off at any time in your device’s settings.

Add to calendar

The sequel trailer can make a small calendar file for a date that matters and hand it to your calendar app. Littleverse doesn’t read your calendar.

5. Ads in the Android app

Android app only

The Android app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, Google’s ad service, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app. You’ll see small banner ads on the Home, Inbox and You screens, and now and then a full-screen ad when you come back from playing a universe. There’s never a full-screen ad when the app opens, and there are no ads while a universe is playing.

What Google collects

To show ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google’s ad software collects:

  • your device’s advertising ID (and a similar app set ID),
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (such as your country or city),
  • device and app information, such as the device model, Android version, language and app version,
  • your interactions with ads, such as when an ad is shown or tapped, and
  • diagnostic information about how the app and the ad software perform.

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and, if you allow it, to personalise the ads you see. Littleverse doesn’t give the ad software your universes, letters, names, photos, songs or gift files.

We ask Google to show only ads suitable for most audiences (rated PG or below).

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you’re in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form before ads are loaded, so you can choose whether Google may use your information for personalised ads. To change your choice later, go to You → Ad privacy choices.

Your advertising ID

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone’s settings. Depending on your phone, it’s under Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads. After you delete it, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an ID, and ads can’t be personalised with it.

Turning ads off

The one-time Remove ads purchase (below) turns the ads off. Once Littleverse knows you own it, it no longer loads or shows ads.

6. The Remove ads purchase

Android app only

Remove ads is a one-time in-app purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment under Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms. We never see your payment details.

Littleverse only asks Google Play whether you own Remove ads, and remembers the answer on your device. If you reinstall the app, erase everything or move to a new phone, tap Restore purchase in You and Google Play will confirm it again.

7. What we don’t do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No servers of our own: nothing you make is uploaded to us.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts or your device’s location, and no access to files other than the ones you pick.

8. The web version

The web version and the iPhone Home Screen app have no ads, no purchases and no analytics. They store everything in your browser on your device, in the same way as described above, and keep working offline.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the technical information your browser sends when it loads the site, such as your IP address, and handles it under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The site loads nothing from any other service.

9. Children

Littleverse is made for adults who want to make something for the people in their lives. It isn’t directed at children under 13 and isn’t designed for kids. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children, and we don’t collect personal information from anyone ourselves.

If you’re a parent or guardian and think a child has used the Android app, you can delete everything it stored with You → Erase everything or by uninstalling it, and reset the device’s advertising ID as described above. You’re also welcome to contact us.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything Littleverse stores stays on your device until you delete it:

  • One universe: open it, tap the … menu and choose Delete universe. A received gift has Remove from inbox on its screen.
  • Everything: go to You → Erase everything. This deletes every universe, received gift, photo, song, setting and bit of progress, and the app starts fresh.
  • Uninstalling the Android app deletes all of its data from your phone. For the web version, clear the site’s data in your browser’s settings.

Gift files you’ve already sent stay with whoever has them. Information Google collects for ads is kept and deleted under Google’s Privacy Policy, and you can manage it with Google’s own tools, such as resetting your advertising ID and My Ad Center. Your Remove ads purchase stays in your Google Play order history.

11. Security

Your universes are kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps can’t read, and are protected by your device’s screen lock. Google’s ad software sends its data over encrypted connections. Gift files aren’t encrypted, so treat them like any photo or document you send. No method of storing or sending information is perfectly secure, but keeping your things on your own device means there’s no server of ours that could leak them.

12. Your choices and rights

Because we don’t hold any of your information, there’s nothing on our side to access, correct or delete: you can view, change and delete everything in the app yourself. For the information Google collects for ads, you can change your consent in You → Ad privacy choices (where it applies), reset or delete your advertising ID, buy Remove ads, or use the rights described in Google’s Privacy Policy. If you have a question or a request, contact us and we’ll help where we can.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how Littleverse handles information, we’ll update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll also mention it in the app’s release notes.
